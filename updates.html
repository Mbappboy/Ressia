Handler creates nation's First Farm

HANDLER, RESSIA — August 2, 2026
Ressia officially has it's first farmland, but tensions may rise with the United States as Remy Handler declared on Sunday that the farmland just outside of Ressia, held by the Unitde States, had been officially claimed by the Ressians.

While this means that now Ressians have their own major source of greens, it also is the most recent part of the trend of growing tensions between the two nations. The farm, located at 207 Ressia Street in Handler, is open to the public on Sundays for volounteer work and vegetable picking.

New Citizenship Regulations

HANDLER, RESSIA — August 1, 2026
The Prime Minister of Ressia has created new citizenship application regulations, to make it easier for non-Seattleites to join the up-and-coming nation. Before the 1st of August, the citizenship laws laid out in the Constitution made it nearly impossible to join the Republic as someone who didn't live within a few miles of the nation.

However, the High Court of Ressia declared this practice unlawful, and ordered the Prime Minister to create new laws. The changes can be veiwed here.

Remy Handler signs Stamp Bill

HANDLER, RESSIA — 7 August 2026
Newly elected Prime Minister Remy Handler made another step in Ressia's history as he signed the nation's first-ever bill yesterday. The bill in question will allocate $32.79 from the Ressian Vault to the Republic's Stamp Budget, which includes official stamps, ink pads, and passport stamps.

Handler made a speach on Thursday about the bill being passed, and, so far, has recieved all-positive opinions on the topic. Handler is looking into other budgets, like the enviroment with Max Handler, and Military with Calvin Handler.
