How to get Citizenship

There are multiple ways to get Ressian citizenship. These are living in Ressia for a year (continuous), being born in Ressia, and applying for citizenship.
The first and last options invlove going to your local Pasrom and asking a worker there about citizenship. You mat be asked to provide a birth certificate or other forms of
identification.When you apply for citizenship without living in Ressia for a year, you have to fill out a form. This will be reviewed by a Pasworker, who will then email
you for an in-person interview. The first option will require you to hire a citizenship lawyer who will present your case in front of a judge in the
High Court of Ressia.

To get a Ressian Passport, you must attend an in-person interview at a Pasrom. The cost of applying for a passport is based on the level of citizenship
you have (Which you can learn more about at a Pasrom). Prior to the interview, you must print out a form. Then, turn this form in along with the
passport fees in an envelope, and request to get your passport photo taken. delivery takes anywhere from a week to 3 months.
