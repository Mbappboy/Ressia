About Ressia

Ressia is a country located in Seattle that values freedom, the environment, and the right to have opionions. We represent this with small things
such as posters warning people about pollution and big things like officialy recognizing the Church of the Flying Spaghetti Monster.Our
government has a Prime Minister, a Parliament, and A Judicial branch.


Official Language

The official languages of Ressia are English and Ressian, a Germanic langauge with very very close ties to English and Noweigian. All official signs,
labels, and monumental plaques in Ressia are written in both languages.


National Holidays

Ressian National holidays include:
Ressia Day, 25 June
Forces Day, 2 July
Phở Day, June 8


Cuisine

Ressian cuisine and Ressia's most valued exports can overlap, with Ressia's highest earning export being vegetables. Hardly any cuisine comes from anywhere
other than nature itself. Enjoy Binpåst, Røsbarmalld, and Vegkombin, served at almost every Ressian resturaunt. If green's aren't your favorite, go to a KwikBar to try some fryed
and grilled food.


Traditional Clothing

Ressian ceremonies always have dress codes for people over 12. At weddings, it is considered a sign of disrespect to not wear a suit or dress. However, any gender
may choose either one, as Ressia doesn't belive that genders whould have seperate dress codes. On Phở Day, it is customary to wear any article of clothing with the word Phở on it.


Interesting Facts

New Zealand, Poland, the Netherlands, and Ressia are some of the only countries to recognize Pastafarianism.
